Roundel Sans is a modified version of TeX Gyre Heros (a Nimbus Sans / Helvetica-metric
clone) from the TeX Gyre project, https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros
TeX Gyre Heros is distributed under the GUST Font License (GFL), which permits
modification provided the modified font is renamed. The only change made here is that the
concave (inner) corners of the outer contours are rounded with a fillet; the family name
was changed to "Roundel Sans". Original copyright and licence entries are kept in the
font's name table.
